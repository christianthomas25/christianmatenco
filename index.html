About

I study Business Analytics and Data Science at IE University in Madrid, building on a Digital Economics and Business background. My work sits where finance, valuation, analytics, strategy, and entrepreneurship overlap.

What I enjoy most is taking messy, unstructured information and turning it into a clear analytical view. I care about understanding how businesses actually make money, how they scale, and how they allocate capital, rather than describing them from the surface. That instinct runs through everything here, from the operating model behind a startup to a valuation engine I built for myself.

02

What I Do

Financial Modelling & Valuation

I build structured models that connect operations, revenue drivers, margins, and cash flow to a valuation. I work in DCF, trading comparables, and sensitivity analysis, and I care about the assumptions holding up under scrutiny, not just the output looking clean.

Business Analytics & Decisions

I use data to find what is actually driving a result. That means regression, clustering, and large-scale data processing when the question needs it, always pointed at a decision rather than analysis for its own sake.

Strategy & Market Analysis

I frame the competitive and commercial picture before recommending anything: where margin pressure comes from, how a market is sized, which moves create defensible position. I have done this both in consulting cases and in a real commercial negotiation.

Startup Finance & Investor Storytelling

I translate operating reality into a finance-led case investors can read: unit economics, ROI scenarios, and a valuation that ties back to the model. The story has to be honest enough to survive diligence, not just pitch well.

03

How I Think

01

Start from the economics

Before models or slides, I ask how the business makes money and what would have to be true for it to make more. The economics anchor everything that follows.

02

Structure before solving

A problem framed well is half solved. I break a question into its parts and decide what actually matters before reaching for an answer, so the work targets the real driver.

03

Data supports judgment

Data sharpens a decision; it does not make it. I use evidence to test and pressure my own view, then take a clear position rather than hiding behind the numbers.
